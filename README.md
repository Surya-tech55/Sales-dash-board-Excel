# Sales-dash-boardd-Excel
  Sales Analytics Dashboard

##  Project Overview

This **Sales Analytics Dashboard** provides an interactive view of sales performance, production costs, profit, profit margin, order quantity, regional performance, monthly trends, yearly sales, and quarterly sales.

The dashboard was designed to identify **sales trends, profitable periods, high-performing regions, and business performance patterns** using interactive filters for **Year, Quarter, and Month**.

## 🔑 Key Performance Indicators

| KPI                 |      Result |
| ------------------- | ----------: |
| **Total Sales**     | **$29.36M** |
| **Production Cost** | **$17.28M** |
| **Profit**          | **$12.08M** |
| **Profit Margin**   |     **41%** |
| **Order Quantity**  |  **60,398** |

## 📈 Key Business Findings

* **Total sales reached $29.36M**, generating approximately **$12.08M profit**.
* The overall **profit margin is 41%**, indicating a strong level of profitability.
* **Production cost was $17.28M**, representing a significant portion of total sales and an important area for cost optimization.
* The dashboard recorded **60,398 total orders**, showing substantial transaction volume.
* **December recorded the highest monthly sales**, at approximately **$3.21M**, indicating strong year-end performance.
* **January recorded comparatively lower sales**, suggesting an opportunity to investigate seasonal demand patterns.
* **2013 was the strongest sales year**, contributing approximately **$16.35M** in sales.
* **Q3 was the strongest financial quarter**, contributing approximately **31% of total sales**, followed by Q2 at **26%**, Q1 at **24%**, and Q4 at **19%**.
* **Australia was the highest-performing region**, contributing approximately **$9.06M** in sales.
* Other notable regional contributors include **Northwest, France, Southeast, Germany, and Canada**.
* The monthly sales trend shows an **overall upward movement toward the end of the year**, with sales peaking in December.

## 🎯 Business Insights

1. **Strong profitability:**
   A 41% profit margin indicates that the business is generating healthy returns from its sales.

2. **Seasonal sales opportunity:**
   The strong performance in December suggests potential seasonal demand. Businesses could increase inventory, marketing, and promotional activities before high-demand periods.

3. **Quarterly performance:**
   Q3 contributes the largest share of sales, making it an important period for sales planning and resource allocation.

4. **Regional concentration:**
   Australia is a major contributor to total sales. Management should analyze successful strategies in high-performing regions and identify opportunities to improve weaker regions.

5. **Cost management:**
   Since production costs account for a substantial portion of sales, controlling production expenses could further improve the existing 41% profit margin.

6. **Year-over-year growth:**
   The significant sales contribution from 2013 compared with earlier years indicates strong business growth during the analyzed period.

## 💡 Conclusion

The dashboard highlights a **profitable and growing sales performance**, with a **41% profit margin**, strong quarterly performance in **Q3**, peak monthly sales in **December**, and **Australia** as the leading region. The analysis can help management make better decisions related to **sales planning, regional strategy, seasonal campaigns, and production-cost optimization**.
